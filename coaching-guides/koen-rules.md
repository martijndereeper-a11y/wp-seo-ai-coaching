# Koen's Coaching Rules — Extracted from Koen OS, System Prompt, and AE Feedback

## Discovery
- Do: Open with a hypothesis about their situation, not "vertel eens over jullie"
- Do: Narrow to ICP and persona early — ask "welke klantgroep is het belangrijkst?"
- Do: Label the pain explicitly — "als ik het goed begrijp is het grootste probleem X"
- Do: Summarize before transitioning to pitch — create agreement first
- Do: Show live in Google/ChatGPT that they are NOT being found
- Don't: Ask questions you could have looked up yourself (team size, role, basic info)
- Don't: Stay in safe information-gathering mode without translating to commercial impact
- Do: Force prioritization — don't accept "everything is important"

## Urgency & Tension
- Do: Create urgency through consequences — "wat kost het jullie als je dit niet oplost?"
- Do: Challenge beliefs that block the deal — don't just validate inefficiency
- Don't: Destabilize with wrong angle — match their concern dimension

## Closing
- Do: Test conviction BEFORE discussing timing — "zijn jullie overtuigd genoeg?"
- Don't: Hand over control with "hoe kan ik jullie helpen?" — propose a concrete step
- Do: Schedule next meeting DURING the call, not after
- Don't: Accept "ik moet erover nadenken" — convert to conditions and next step
- Do: Frame next meeting as decision moment, not exploration
- Do: Use three-step close: (1) excited? (2) reason not to? (3) who decides?
- Do: Compress vague timeframes — "wat verandert er over 2 maanden dat we nu niet weten?"

## Objection Handling
- Do: Ask "waarom vraag je dat?" before answering prospect questions
- Do: Stack ALL objections before solving any — map them first
- Don't: Minimize concerns — don't say "dat is maar 1 uur per maand"
- Don't: Jump to pricing before conviction is established
- Do: Unpack "te duur" — ask what expensive means to them first
- Do: Treat "niet het juiste moment" as conviction problem, not calendar problem
- Do: Treat "ik moet intern overleggen" as doubt signal — probe what's missing

## Demo & Pitch
- Do: Connect every explanation back to their stated pain — "omdat je zei dat..."
- Don't: Let the pitch become a 10-15 minute monologue — check in every few minutes
- Do: Explain the content backlog problem to neutralize "we have ChatGPT"
- Do: Frame agency partnership, not replacement — "de agency wordt waardevoller"

## Language & Authority
- Don't: Use softening words excessively — misschien, eigenlijk, een beetje, zeg maar, volgens mij
- Don't: Use hypothetical language where directness is needed
- Do: Amplify prospect's positive statements — "precies! dat is exact waarom dit relevant is"

## Process Control
- Don't: Let prospect lead the conversation structure — redirect when needed
- Don't: Propose next steps before understanding the decision process
- Do: Confirm your answer resolved the question before moving on
- Do: Set a strong upfront contract — two outcomes: no fit (fine) or fit (next step)
- Don't: Ramble after answering — return control
- Don't: Mention your inexperience

## Follow-up
- Do: Offer to speak with the real decision maker directly
- Do: Recap email same day + 1-2 day window + scheduled decision call
